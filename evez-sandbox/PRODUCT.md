# EVEZ Sandbox: product kernel

This is the economic/product boundary, not a quest system.

## What makes it commercially distinct

Current Upwork marketplace data shows "AI automation" remained its most-searched AI term in August 2026, with AI automation expert, AI workflow automation, and AI automation engineer among the fastest-growing AI searches. The opportunity is not another chat UI. It is an environment where businesses can inspect and rehearse autonomous workflows before exposing them to production.

## Sellable modes

### 1. Workflow World
Import a business process and generate a living simulation of the people, systems, queues, exceptions, costs, permissions, and failure modes.

### 2. Agent Society
Deploy multiple specialized agents into a sandbox and measure cooperation, conflict, tool usage, evidence quality, and economic output before production.

### 3. Digital Twin Audit
Connect authorized business data and reconstruct a time-bounded operational model. Never silently infer access.

### 4. Capability Forge
Let an agent propose a tool/workflow. Test it against simulated conditions, then promote it to a controlled production bridge.

### 5. Evidence Room
Every decision receives provenance: input, actor, tool, source, timestamp, output, confidence, and consequence.

### 6. Scenario Market
Package reusable workflow worlds for industries such as agencies, property management, e-commerce operations, local service businesses, support desks, recruiting, and internal IT.

## Revenue architecture

Do not promise guaranteed income. Price measurable deployment value.

- paid diagnostic: fixed-fee workflow simulation
- implementation: one-time setup
- managed world: recurring subscription
- production bridge: usage-based infrastructure
- private worlds: enterprise contract
- scenario packs: reusable licenses

The product should optimize for retained customer value, not token volume.

## Acquisition loop

PUBLIC DEMO -> DISCOVERY -> IMPORT WORKFLOW -> SIMULATE -> REPORT -> PAID PILOT -> PRODUCTION BRIDGE -> MONTHLY MANAGEMENT

The demo must show the system discovering a non-obvious bottleneck without fake metrics.

## Safety/evidence boundary

No autonomous financial transfer, account takeover, credential collection, destructive infrastructure change, or unauthorized data access. The sandbox may simulate these actions as hypothetical events, clearly labeled as simulated.
